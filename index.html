Workshops
In the previous years we had workshops on amongst others cryptography, mobile security, and reversing. The workshops for next year will be published in December this year.

Capture The Flag
We have a small CTF competition for smashing the stack & fun. In a CTF, the goal is to obtain the 'flag' by hacking into a vulnerable system. For example, in web challenges, you may need to exploit a website's reflection of user-supplied input to obtain the flag. Whereas in cryptography, you have to break the encryption to obtain the flag.

The flag is a string of text that you submit to the competition platform to score points. It usually looks something like this: FLAG{4N_ExaMplE_fl4G}

FAQ
Can I sign up for only the workshops or only the CTF?
Yes you aboslutely can! Preference is given to female students who sign up for the entire day.

I identify as a woman, but I am not a student. Can I still sign up?
Definitely, we do not have a strict cut-off. In the previous events we accepted participants from high schoolers to women looking for a career change. While most participants are college and university students, we welcome participants of all ages. Preference is given to female students, so keep in mind that most participants will be roughly between the age of 18-25.

Lunch?
We will try to arrange lunch, but no promises. We accommodate allergies. However, in cases of extreme allergies that can result in emergency situations, we request that you pack your own lunch.

Language?
The main language throughout the day will be English. There will be Dutch-speaking volunteers available to help translate when necessary.
